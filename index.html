Adventure Plus

Gather, build and craft, then climb, glide and fight your way across Hearthvale and the windswept Gale Steppes.

Playing in the browser

  • Works best in Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer. The first load takes a little while (about 12 MB).
  • On a computer: click Play, then use the mouse to look and WASD to move. Esc pauses. A game controller works too.
  • On a phone: turn it sideways. Tap once and the game goes full screen with on-screen controls.
  • Your world saves in the browser on that device, so come back with the same browser to continue.

Installing on an Android phone

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. When it finishes, tap the downloaded file (or find AdventurePlus.apk in your Downloads).
  3. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns that the app is from an unknown developer, tap More details then Install anyway. It is a game made by a friend, not from the Play Store.

To update later, download it again and install over the old one. Your phone world is kept.
